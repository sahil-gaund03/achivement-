## Flagship Project — Bank Customer Churn Prediction

<div align="center">

[![View Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/View%20Repository-181717?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/sahil-gaund03/churn-prediction)
[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live%20Demo-FF4B4B?style=for-the-badge&logo=streamlit&logoColor=white)](https://churn-prediction.streamlit.app)
[![Open in Colab](https://img.shields.io/badge/Open%20in%20Colab-F9AB00?style=for-the-badge&logo=googlecolab&logoColor=black)](https://colab.research.google.com/)

</div>

### Problem Statement

> Banks lose **15–25% of customers annually** to churn — costing millions in replacement acquisition costs. Most internal retention tools rely on lagging indicators and react after the customer has already left.
>
> **Goal:** A real-time, interpretable ML system that flags at-risk customers before they leave — deployable by non-technical bank staff, with full SHAP explainability for every individual prediction.

---

### System Architecture

```
Raw Bank Dataset (10,000+ records)
        |
        v
Preprocessing (Imputation · Encoding · Scaling)
        |
        v
EDA (Correlation · Distribution · Outlier Analysis)
        |
        v
Feature Engineering
  - Credit Score Bins
  - Balance-to-Salary Ratio
  - Tenure Ratio
  - IsActiveMember Flag
        |
        v
Model Training (5-Fold Cross-Validation)
  +-----------------------+
  | Logistic Regression   |  81.2%
  | Random Forest         |  84.6%
  | SVM                   |  82.1%
  | LightGBM              |  85.9%
  | XGBoost (BEST)        |  87.3%  <---
  +-----------------------+
        |
        v
SHAP Explainability (Waterfall · Beeswarm · Summary)
        |
        v
Streamlit Dashboard
  - Real-time single-row inference
  - CSV batch upload & scoring
        |
        v
Bank Relationship Manager
```

---

### ML Methodology

| Phase | Actions | Tools |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Data Wrangling** | Missing value imputation, outlier detection (IQR), type casting | `Pandas` · `NumPy` |
| **EDA** | Churn rate by geography, age cohort, balance distribution, correlation heatmaps | `Matplotlib` · `Seaborn` |
| **Feature Engineering** | Credit score bins, balance-to-salary ratio, tenure ratio, activity flags | `Pandas` · `Scikit-learn` |
| **Modeling** | 5 algorithms, k=5 stratified cross-validation, hyperparameter tuning | `Scikit-learn` · `XGBoost` · `LightGBM` |
| **Explainability** | Per-prediction SHAP waterfall, global beeswarm, summary plots | `SHAP` |
| **Deployment** | Interactive web app, single-row and CSV batch inference | `Streamlit` |

---

### Model Performance

<div align="center">

| Model | Accuracy | ROC-AUC | F1-Score | Precision | Recall |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Logistic Regression | 81.2% | 0.774 | 0.76 | 0.79 | 0.73 |
| SVM | 82.1% | 0.798 | 0.79 | 0.81 | 0.77 |
| Random Forest | 84.6% | 0.842 | 0.83 | 0.86 | 0.80 |
| LightGBM | 85.9% | 0.861 | 0.84 | 0.87 | 0.82 |
| **XGBoost** | **87.3%** | **0.891** | **0.86** | **0.89** | **0.84** |

</div>

**SHAP-Derived Insights:**

- `Age` and `NumOfProducts` were the **strongest global churn predictors**
- Customers in **Germany** showed **2.5x higher** churn propensity vs. France and Spain
- Inactive members with `Balance > $100K` had a **68% individual churn probability**
- `IsActiveMember` binary flag had outsized impact in borderline classification cases
